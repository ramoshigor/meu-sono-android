Keep a private sleep log on your Android device.

Features:
• Start and stop a sleep timer, or enter a night manually.
• Review and edit your sleep history, notes, and sleep-quality ratings.
• Track a daily sleep goal, averages, bedtime regularity, and charts.
• Set a bedtime reminder based on your wake-up time and sleep goal.
• Choose light, dark, or system theme.
• Optionally keep an active sleep session running in the background with a persistent notification.
• Export CSV files and back up or restore data as JSON.

No account is required. The app has no internet permission; records stay on your device. Insights describe recorded data and are not medical advice or a diagnosis.
